Luis Paredes

Luis Paredes is a writer, artist, and bookseller.

His genre-blending work appears in Tangled Web Magazine, the Kaidankai Horror Podcast, Crow & Cross Keys, and Tall Tale TV.

Luis self-published two books, Out On a Limb and Headhunters which are available online and in bookstores.

He is currently working on the sequels to Out On a Limb and a new, full-length horror novel.
